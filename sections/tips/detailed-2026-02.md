# What are Tips?

## Built-In Help System

A smart contextual help feature that provides:

- 💡 **Context-Specific Guidance** - Tips relevant to what you're doing
- 🎯 **Global Tips Panel** - Access all tips from one place
- 🚀 **Auto-Show for New Users** - Essential tips appear automatically
- 🔍 **Searchable** - Find tips quickly
- ✅ **Dismissible** - Hide tips you've learned

::right::

## Access Tips

<div class="space-y-4">

**Global Panel:**
- Click the 💡 lightbulb icon in header
- Browse all tips by category
- Search for specific help

**Contextual Tips:**
- Look for ❓ help icons
- Click to see relevant tips
- Positioned near related features
